1. Who we are

Shiv Kailasa is a residential township by Om Shivam Buildcon Pvt. Ltd., located opposite AIIMS and IIM Nagpur in MIHAN, Nagpur. Sales and marketing for the project are handled by Blue Sparrow Properties Pvt. Ltd., an authorised partner of Om Shivam Buildcon Pvt. Ltd.

In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean Om Shivam Buildcon Pvt. Ltd. and Blue Sparrow Properties Pvt. Ltd., who together decide how your information is used for enquiries made through this website. The project is registered with MahaRERA: Phase I – P50500018424, Phase II – P50500029945.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Enquiry and site visit forms: your name, mobile number, email address and any message you write.
  • “Is Shiv Kailasa right for you?” quiz: your answers to the quick questions, such as the home size and budget you are considering.
  • Chat and WhatsApp: your phone number, profile name and the messages you send us.
  • Calls and visits: details you share when our team speaks with you or when you visit the site.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, operating system, screen size and language.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Pages viewed, time spent, referring website or advertisement, and the buttons you tap.

We do not ask for bank, card, Aadhaar or PAN details through this website. Please do not send them in the enquiry form or chat.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and share pricing, floor plans and availability.
  • To book, confirm and manage your site visit.
  • To follow up by call, SMS, email or WhatsApp about Shiv Kailasa.
  • To understand how the website is used and to improve it.
  • To measure whether our advertising is working.
  • To keep the website secure and to meet legal and regulatory obligations, including under RERA.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Consent and lawful use

We process your personal data with your consent, which you give when you submit a form, start a chat or message us on WhatsApp. We may also process it where the law permits, for example to comply with a legal obligation. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000, along with the rules made under them.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us using the details in section 12. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already took place.

By submitting an enquiry, you agree that we may contact you even if your number is registered on the Do Not Disturb (DND/NDNC) list, because you asked us to get in touch. Tell us if you would rather not be contacted and we will stop.

6. Cookies and analytics

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use them to keep the site working, remember your quiz progress, count visits, and measure advertising. We may use tools such as Google Analytics / Meta Pixel / Google Ads — edit to match what is installed.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work properly if you do.

This website may link to or open third-party services such as WhatsApp, Google Maps or YouTube. Their privacy practices are their own, and we encourage you to read them.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for as long as we need it to deal with your interest in Shiv Kailasa, and for up to 24 months after our last contact if you do not buy. If you become a customer, we keep your records for the period required by law. After that, we delete or anonymise the data.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limited internal access, to protect your information. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Correct or update data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Erase your data, unless we must keep it by law.
  • Stop sending you calls, messages or emails.
  • Withdraw your consent.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you are unable to.

To make a request, write to us at the address in section 12. We may need to confirm your identity first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

10. Children

This website is meant for adults looking to buy property. We do not knowingly collect personal data of anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us their information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If we make a significant change, we will post a notice on the website.
